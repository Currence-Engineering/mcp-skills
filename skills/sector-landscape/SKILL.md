# Sector Landscape

Get a smart outsider up to speed on a market in one page. Requires the Currence MCP.

## Steps
1. Identify the sector. If the name is broad, pick the closest Currence solution landscape and say which one you chose.
2. Ask who the output is for only if it changes the answer (for example an investment committee, a conference, a slide). Otherwise default to a one-page brief.
3. Read Currence research first: the sector compass for that sector, then the latest outlook and any analyst takes. The compass is the spine of the landscape.
4. Pull the data:
   - Leading companies in the sector, with stage and total raised.
   - Deals in the last 12 months: count, total and notable rounds.
   - Active projects, if the sector has deployments.
   - Policies and market events from the last 90 days.
5. For technology readiness, open the tech profiles for the main technologies. They carry Currence's readiness scoring.
6. If the user is weighing whether to enter the market, add the three hardest barriers to entry, sourced from research, policy and project data.
7. Write the landscape in this order:
   - **The one-line view**: what is true about this market right now.
   - **Drivers and limiters**: three each, from Currence research, not general knowledge.
   - **Value chain**: where value accrues and where the bottlenecks sit.
   - **Who is leading**: 5 to 8 companies, with stage and why they matter.
   - **Capital**: deal count and notable rounds in the last 12 months.
   - **Key questions**: the three questions that decide how this market plays out.
   - **Signposts**: three things to watch over the next two quarters.
8. End with Currence platform links to the compass, the leading companies and the main projects.

## Tips
- For grid storage questions use the long-duration storage compass. It covers all durations and benchmarks against lithium-ion.
- For policy, search events and policies by keyword before saying nothing has happened.
- If the market looks crowded, check adjacent sectors in the taxonomy and say what you found.

## Limits
- Only quote a market size if it comes from Currence research, and cite it. Do not invent one.
- Policy coverage is strongest for data center siting. For other sectors, say policy coverage is thin and cite what exists.
- No power prices, nodal or LMP data.

## Format
Headline states the insight. Short sections. Numbers as $1.2bn and 48.3 GW. Every figure gets a source. Offer a slide-ready version at the end.

## Generic AI gives everyone the same answer

Ask a general-purpose assistant about geothermal, SMRs or data center power and you get what everyone else gets: answers from a stale training cutoff, familiar names picked by reputation, and nothing you can check.

The Currence MCP connects your assistant to the same expert-validated research and data our clients use on the Currence platform: companies, investors, deals, projects, contracts, policies, market events, sector compasses and tech profiles across the energy and AI buildout.

Skills make that connection useful from day one. Each one tells your assistant which Currence data to check first, how to weigh it, and what the finished output should look like. No long prompts. No prompt engineering.

> **Same question, two answers.** *"Who's investing in geothermal right now? Show me the biggest deals and the most active investors from the last 12 months."*
>
> **On its own**, the assistant hedges with "as of my last update," recalls older rounds and names familiar investors by reputation.
>
> **With Currence**, it returns live deal data covering 35 geothermal deals since October 2025, ranks investors by actual deal count, lists full syndicates with leads flagged, and links every company and deal to the source.

## Get started in five minutes

### 1. Connect the Currence MCP

| Assistant | Steps |
|---|---|
| **Claude** | Settings → Connectors → Add custom connector. Name it `Currence`, paste `https://mcp.currence.ai`, click Add, then Connect. Log in with your Currence account and click Authorize. |
| **ChatGPT** | Settings → Apps & Connectors → Advanced settings → turn on Developer mode. Click Create, name it `Currence`, paste `https://mcp.currence.ai`. Log in and click Authorize. |
| **Microsoft Copilot** | If your company manages Copilot centrally, your IT admin may need to add the Currence connector for you. |

### 2. Get the skills you want

Each skill is a single `SKILL.md` file. Open one from the [table above](#the-skills) and click the download icon (**Download raw file**) at the top right of the file. Or copy its contents.

Pick the ones that fit your work. You don't need all five.

### 3. Add them to your assistant

The quickest way: drag the `SKILL.md` into a chat, or paste its contents, and ask your assistant to save it as a skill.

Or upload by hand:

- **Claude:** Customize → Skills → Create New Skill → Upload a Skill
- **ChatGPT:** Skills → New Skill → Upload from your computer

Then just ask. Your assistant picks the right skill from your question.

## Get better answers

- **Ask for platform links.** Add *"include Currence platform links"* to any prompt and click through to the full company, project, deal or report.
- **Name Currence.** If you have several connectors, say *"use Currence"* so your assistant pulls our data.
- **Say who it's for.** *"For an investment committee"* or *"for a slide"* changes the depth and the format.
- **Start with research for judgment calls.** For *"who's winning?"* or *"what matters?"*, ask it to check Currence research first. For counts and lists, ask for the data directly.
- **Not sure where to start?** Ask *"What can the Currence MCP do?"*

## FAQ

**I can't add the skills.**
Skills need the Currence MCP connected first. Follow [step 1](#1-connect-the-currence-mcp), then add the skills.

**Which assistants are supported?**
Any assistant that supports custom MCP connectors, including Claude, ChatGPT and Microsoft Copilot.

**What data can I reach?**
The same research and data you can see on the Currence platform under your subscription.

**What's the difference between the MCP and the Currence API?**
The MCP is for individuals working inside their AI assistant. The API is for teams building structured, repeatable workflows or bringing Currence data into their own systems.

**Can I edit a skill?**
Yes. Each skill is a plain-text `SKILL.md` file in the [`skills/`](skills) folder. Copy it, change the steps or format to match how your team works, and upload your version.

**Want help fitting these into your team's workflow?**
Your account manager can walk you through the workflows that work best for teams like yours.
